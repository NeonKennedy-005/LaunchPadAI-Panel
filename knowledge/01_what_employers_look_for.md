# What employers look for (internships & entry-level)

![Skills employers emphasize](/career-sources/employers-look-for.png)

## Snapshot (NACE Job Outlook research)

When reviewing college student resumes for early-career roles, employers repeatedly look for **evidence** — not buzzwords — of:

1. **Problem solving** (~90% of employers in NACE Job Outlook reporting)
2. **Teamwork** (~80%)
3. **Written communication**
4. **Initiative**
5. **Strong work ethic**
6. **Technical skills** relevant to the role
7. **Verbal communication**
8. **Flexibility / adaptability**
9. **Analytical / quantitative skills**

Sources:
- [What Are Employers Looking for When Reviewing College Students’ Resumes? (NACE)](https://www.naceweb.org/talent-acquisition/candidate-selection/what-are-employers-looking-for-when-reviewing-college-students-resumes)
- [Job Outlook 2026 Spring Update — key skills (NACE)](https://naceweb.org/research/reports/2026/job-outlook/spring-update)

## Skills-based hiring is growing

- Roughly **~70%** of surveyed employers report using skills-based hiring (NACE Job Outlook 2026).
- Skills show up most in **interviews** and **screening**; GPA screening has declined vs. prior years.
- Students win when they **translate** coursework, clubs, jobs, and projects into workplace skills with examples.

Sources:
- [Employer Use of Skills-Based Hiring Practices Grows (NACE)](https://www.naceweb.org/job-market/trends-and-predictions/employer-use-of-skills-based-hiring-practices-grows)
- [Skills-Based Hiring Grows, but College Students Don’t Fully Understand It (NACE)](https://www.naceweb.org/about-us/press/2026/skills-based-hiring-grows-but-college-students-dont-fully-understand-it)

## NACE Career Readiness competencies (shared language)

NACE defines career readiness through core competencies such as:

- Career & Self-Development
- Communication
- Critical Thinking
- Leadership
- Professionalism
- Teamwork
- Technology
- (Equity & Inclusion competency exists in the framework; campuses/employers should follow current legal guidance on use)

Source / definitions: [NACE Career Readiness Defined](https://www.naceweb.org/career-readiness/competencies/career-readiness-defined/)

## Experiential learning matters

Employers highly value:

- U.S.-based **internships**
- **Co-ops**
- On-campus student work / apprenticeships (also valued by many)

Implication for students: get experience **and** write bullets that show skills used on the job.

## Practical resume takeaways for LaunchPadAI users

- Lead with **action + context + tool + result** (only real metrics).
- Mirror must-have skills from the JD **only if you can prove them**.
- Prefer one strong internship/project story over a long list of vague duties.
- Prepare STAR examples for teamwork, conflict, failure, and problem solving before interviews.
