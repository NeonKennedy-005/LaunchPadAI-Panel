# Campus recruiting — how universities and companies hire early talent

![Campus recruiting timeline](/career-sources/campus-recruiting-timeline.png)

## How employers recruit college talent (general pattern)

From NACE recruiting benchmarks and Job Outlook reporting:

- Most early-talent employers still rely heavily on **direct campus recruiting** (career fairs, info sessions, campus interviews).
- **Career services partnerships** matter: branding on campus, faculty/department relationships, and campus events.
- Recruiting is no longer “fall only” — a meaningful share of full-time and internship hiring continues into **spring**.
- Offer timing can stretch; students often decide within about **1–2 weeks** once an offer arrives.

Sources:
- [NACE 2025 Recruiting Benchmarks Report (executive summary)](https://naceweb.org/docs/default-source/default-document-library/2025/publication/executive-summary/nace-2025-recruiting-benchmarks-report-executive-summary.pdf?sfvrsn=c0e2a5c1_5)
- [Job Outlook 2026 Spring Update](https://naceweb.org/research/reports/2026/job-outlook/spring-update)

## Channels students should use (in priority order for most undergrads)

1. **Handshake** (campus postings + employer events)
2. **University career services** (advising, fairs, employer info sessions)
3. **LinkedIn** (alumni + recruiter outreach; Easy Apply carefully)
4. **Official company university / students pages** (applications + program timelines)
5. **Faculty, clubs, and prior supervisors** (referrals and recommendations)

## Student playbook

- Map your **fall fair → apply → screen → interview → offer** timeline on a calendar.
- Treat company “University” pages as source of truth for deadlines.
- After every fair: 24-hour follow-up note + Handshake/LinkedIn connection.
- Track everything: Wishlist → Applied → Interview → Offer.
